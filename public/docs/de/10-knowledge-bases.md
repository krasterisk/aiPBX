# Knowledge Bases

> Upload company documents (FAQ, price lists, policies). The assistant answers from your files instead of guessing.

## How to open

Menu → **AI Bots** → **Knowledge Bases**.

![Knowledge bases](/docs/screenshots/knowledge-base.png)

## Steps

1. Create a base and upload PDF/DOCX/TXT files.
2. Wait until indexing shows **Ready**.
3. In [assistant settings](./02-assistants.md), select this base.
4. Test in [Playground](./05-playground.md).
