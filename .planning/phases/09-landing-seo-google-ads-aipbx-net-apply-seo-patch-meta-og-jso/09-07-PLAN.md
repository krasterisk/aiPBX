<objective>
Make bots see fully-rendered meta/OG/JSON-LD (D-04, GAP-40) by prerendering the 4 public
routes to static HTML at webpack build time — using `@prerenderer/webpack-plugin` +
`@prerenderer/renderer-puppeteer` (NOT react-snap, per D-04), waiting on the
`seo-render-ready` event dispatched by the landing pages (09-04). Add a post-build grep
gate (`scripts/verify-prerender.js`) covering D-03/D-04/D-05.

Purpose: the SPA injects meta client-side only; without prerender the Google Ads quality
bot and social scrapers see an empty `#root`. This plan is the core SEO enabler and the
riskiest (hydration with MUI/framer-motion/lazy — RESEARCH A1), hence a legitimacy
checkpoint + a build smoke.
Output: prerender plugin config + verify script + package.json wiring.
</objective>
